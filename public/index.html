Phone / Android
Open the mobile app: https://trihealth-livedesk-updates.onrender.com/m/
Then Chrome menu → Install app or Add to Home screen. The phone app has the same Chart tab (Desk and Scanner stay there too).

Current version: 1.2.5 · version.json · positions.json · scan.json

Downloads: TriHealth-LiveDesk.exe · console

The Windows app checks version.json at startup and every 10 minutes, verifies SHA-256, swaps the exe, and restarts. Positions refresh from positions.json about every 30 seconds. Scanner pulls scan.json about every 60 seconds.

Coins map to Binance USDT. Stocks pass through (SHOP.TO opens on the TSX). Paste BINANCE:BTCUSDT or NASDAQ:AAPL to override.
